SubHero Guidelines

The everyday rules for using SubHero safely • Derived from our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

These guidelines are the practical, plain-language version of our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Read them once and you'll know exactly how SubHero works — and how to keep your money and data safe.

1. Using Your Account

  • You must be at least 18 years old, or have the consent of a parent or guardian.
  • Give us accurate details when you register and complete verification. Wrong details can delay or block your services.
  • Keep your password and 4-digit transaction PIN private. You are responsible for everything that happens on your account.
  • SubHero staff will never ask you for your password or PIN. Anyone who does is not us — block and report them.

2. Funding Your Wallet

  • Fund via card, bank transfer or your dedicated virtual account, processed securely by our licensed payment provider (Squad / GTBank).
  • Deposit fees: ₦20 below ₦1,000 and ₦50 for ₦1,000 and above.
  • Your balance is for buying services on SubHero. It is not transferable to other users and can't be withdrawn as cash unless we approve it as a refund.

3. Buying the Right Service

  • VTU purchases — airtime, data, cable TV and electricity — are processed instantly once you submit the order.
  • Double-check the destination. If you enter the wrong phone, meter or decoder number, the purchase cannot be reversed or refunded.
  • If a network provider fails or declines an order, we automatically refund the exact amount back to your wallet balance.

4. Social Media (SMM) Orders

  • SMM services are delivered automatically through third-party provider APIs. Delivery speed varies with network traffic and platform updates.
  • Don't order boosts for private profiles, restricted links, or anything that breaks Nigerian law or a platform's own terms.

5. What We Collect, and Why

We only collect what we need to run and protect the platform:

  • Account details — full name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Identity details (KYC) — BVN, date of birth, gender and address, required to issue your dedicated virtual account under CBN / GTBank Squad rules.
  • Transaction records — deposits, airtime and data destinations, SMM orders and wallet balances.
  • Technical data — IP address, device, browser and session logs, used for security and fraud prevention.

We use this to fulfil your orders, manage your wallet and virtual account, verify your identity against money-laundering and fraud risk, and send receipts, security alerts and support messages.

6. How We Protect Your Data

  • Data in transit is protected with SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption, and database writes use bank-grade row locking.
  • Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 (SHA-256). We never store raw card numbers or banking PINs.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your data. We share it only with: licensed payment providers (Squad / HabariPay / GTCO), telecom vendor APIs (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) for delivery, and Nigerian courts, the CBN or law enforcement where the law requires it.

7. Your Privacy Rights (NDPR)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation you can:

  • Access, review or request a copy of the data we hold on you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Request account closure and data erasure, subject to financial record retention laws.

8. What's Not Allowed

  • Fraudulent chargebacks, or using stolen cards or bank accounts.
  • Scripts, bots or scrapers used to exploit giveaways or our endpoints.
  • Attempting to breach, test or reverse-engineer SubHero systems.

Accounts that break these rules are suspended immediately, and unverified funds are reported to financial regulators.

9. Refunds, Faults & Liability

  • Third-party network outages — MTN, Airtel, Glo or 9mobile downtime, or bank transfer delays — are outside our control and are not treated as our fault.
  • Our maximum liability for any dispute is limited to the value of the specific transaction in question.
  • These guidelines are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, with disputes handled by arbitration or the courts of Lagos State.
